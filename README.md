A professional Google Sheets / Excel template for content creators to track revenue across multiple income streams, manage expenses, and view an auto-calculating financial dashboard.

## What's Inside

### 📊 4 Sheets

1. **Setup** — Configure your currency, tax rate, and 10 platform names
2. **Income** — Track payments from YouTube, sponsorships, Patreon, affiliates, digital products, merch, consulting, speaking, donations, and more (60 rows, 10 sample entries)
3. **Expenses** — Log business expenses across 15 categories with tax deductibility flags (60 rows, 10 sample entries)
4. **Dashboard** — Auto-calculating financial overview

- **8 KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit, Profit Margin, Est. Quarterly Tax, After-Tax Income, Avg Monthly Income, Active Income Streams

- **Quarterly Tax Schedule:** Auto-calculated tax estimates per quarter

- **Tax Deductibility Tracking:** Each expense is flagged as deductible or not — the dashboard knows what counts
- **Auto-Calculating Formulas:** All metrics update automatically when you enter new data

## How to Use

1. Open the spreadsheet in Google Sheets or Excel
2. Go to the **Setup** sheet and configure your currency and tax rate
3. Enter your income in the **Income** sheet (date, platform, amount, etc.)
4. Log expenses in the **Expenses** sheet
5. Check the **Dashboard** for auto-calculated financial insights
